Uninstall Cloudflare WARP

info Uninstalling this component will completely remove Cloudflare WARP from your computer.

To safely uninstall Cloudflare WARP, follow these steps:

  1. Open Windows Settings (press Win + I).
  2. Go to Apps → Installed apps.
  3. Find «Cloudflare WARP» in the list.
  4. Click the three dots (⋯) and select «Uninstall».
  5. Follow the Windows uninstaller prompts to complete removal.
